ⓘ Analysis Methodology ⌄

PULSAR compares time-aligned crew health observations with shared spacecraft environmental measurements.

01. Data Collection

Physiological measurements, reported symptoms and habitat sensor observations.

02. Data Synchronization

Comparison of valid observations within a shared time window and habitat.

03. Pattern Detection

Identification of similar observations among multiple crew members.

04. Environmental Comparison

Evaluation of corresponding environmental changes.

05. Risk Awareness

Presentation of possible shared risk patterns for human review.

06. Data Quality

Missing or insufficient data are not interpreted as normal health conditions.

Spaceflight Research Context

Human health beyond Earth

Spaceflight introduces unique challenges associated with altered gravity, radiation, isolation, distance from Earth and closed spacecraft environments. Monitoring crew health together with environmental conditions supports mission-specific health awareness.
